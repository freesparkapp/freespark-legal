FreeSpark Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026
Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FreeSpark collects, uses, shares, and protects information about you when you use the FreeSpark mobile app and related services (together, the "Service"). When you sign up, you are asked to check a box that you agree to our Terms of Use and have read this Privacy Policy.

FreeSpark is operated by Brandon Wells, based in Hawaii, USA ("FreeSpark," "we," "us," or "our"). You can reach us at freesparkapp@gmail.com. Notices to FreeSpark must be sent by email to freesparkapp@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • FreeSpark is free for members. There are no subscriptions, boosts, premium tiers, or in-app purchases.
  • We pay for the Service by showing promotions from local businesses. These are always labeled "Sponsored ad." Ads never change who you see or who you match with.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We do not use third-party ad networks or tracking tools, and we do not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) or track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Advertisers only receive aggregate numbers (how many times an ad was shown and tapped). They never receive your name, profile, messages, or other personal information.
  • FreeSpark uses only the city you type in your profile. The app never asks for or reads your device's location.
  • An automatic content filter checks text you submit and blocks insults, explicit sexual requests, and money-scam wording. Blocked text is not shown to anyone else but is saved for safety review.
  • FreeSpark is for adults 18 and older only.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile → Settings → Delete account.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

Account information
- Email address and password. Your password is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth) and stored in hashed form. We cannot see your password.
- Date of birth, used to check that you are at least 18 and to show your age (not your full birth date) on your profile.
- Your agreement, at sign-up, to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

Profile information (much of this is shown to other members)
- Display name.
- Gender and dating preferences, such as dating intentions and the age range you want to see.
- Bio, profile prompts and your answers, and interests.
- Profile photos.
- Your city (for example, "Honolulu"), which you type in yourself. See Section 2.3.

Photo verification, if you choose it
- If you choose to verify your profile, you take a selfie copying a pose we show you (for example, a wave or a peace sign). This selfie is stored privately and is seen only by FreeSpark's administrator for manual review. It is never shown on your profile or to other members. The selfie file is deleted when the review is finished. We keep the result (approved or rejected), the pose requested, review dates, and any short review note.
- Verification is done by a person comparing the selfie to your profile photos. We do not use facial recognition software, and we do not create or store face templates, face geometry, or other biometric identifiers from your photos.

Messages and activity with other members
- Messages you send to and receive from your matches.
- Likes, passes, and matches.
- Plans and activities you create, join, or save (for example, a title, description, city, venue, date and time, and capacity), and your attendance or waitlist status.
- Businesses and activities you save, and "Plan your first date" suggestions you send in a chat.

Safety information
- Reports you submit (the reason and any details you write) and reports others submit about you.
- People you block, and people who have blocked you.

Settings
- Notification preferences (matches, likes, messages).
- Read receipt setting (you can turn read receipts off).
- Discovery filters, such as maximum distance and interests.
- Appearance setting (light, dark, or match iPhone), stored on your device.

Sponsorship inquiries
- If you or a business uses the in-app "Advertise your business on FreeSpark" form, we collect the business name, contact name, phone number, email address, website, business category, city, and the message you write.

Communications with us
- If you email us, we keep your message and our reply.

2.2 Information created when you use the Service

  • Message status. When a message was sent and, if read receipts are on, when it was read.
  • Content filter results. FreeSpark automatically checks the text you submit — messages, display names, bios, interests, profile prompt answers, and plan titles and descriptions — both in the app and again on our servers. The check looks for insults, explicit sexual requests, and money-scam wording. If text is blocked, it is not posted or shown to any other member. Instead, we save the blocked text along with your account ID, where you tried to post it (for example, a message or your bio), the reason it was flagged, and the time, so FreeSpark's administrator can review it for safety. Text that passes the check is handled like any other content you post. The filter is automatic and rule-based; a person reviews saved blocked text, and the filter alone does not suspend accounts.
  • Typing indicators. A short-lived signal that you are typing in a chat. It is shown only to the other person in that chat and is not kept as a history.
  • Push notification token. If you allow notifications, Apple gives your device a push token. We store it so we can send you alerts through the Apple Push Notification service (APNs). We also keep a short record of notifications queued for delivery.
  • In-app notifications. Records of match, like, and message alerts shown inside the app.
  • Sponsored ad events. When a sponsored ad is shown to you or you tap it, we record the ad, your account ID, your profile city, the placement (for example, Discover or chat list), and the time. We use this to limit how often you see the same ad, to prevent fake or duplicate counts, and to create aggregate statistics for advertisers. Advertisers receive only totals, never this event-level record.
  • Limited usage events. The app may record basic product events (for example, that a feature was used) linked to your account to help us operate and improve the Service. These events are designed not to include message content, sensitive profile details, precise location, or the contents of safety reports.
  • Technical and security information. Our hosting provider may automatically log technical information, such as IP address, device and app version, and request times, for security, fraud prevention, and troubleshooting.

2.3 Location

  • FreeSpark uses only the city you type in your profile (for example, "Honolulu"), and any city you enter for a plan.
  • The app never requests or reads your device's location (no GPS, Wi-Fi, or other device location data), and it does not ask for location permission.
  • We estimate approximate distances between city centers to support the distance filter and to show local plans, businesses, and ads for your city.

2.4 Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect payment information from members (FreeSpark has no purchases).
  • We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • We do not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking software development kits (SDKs).
  • We do not access your device's location.
  • We do not read your contacts or other apps' data.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  1. Run the Service: create your account, show your profile, show you people to like or pass, create matches, deliver messages, run plans and activities, and send the notifications you asked for.
  2. Respect your settings: apply your age range, distance, and interest filters, blocks, read-receipt setting, and notification choices.
  3. Keep members safe: check that members are 18+, automatically filter text for insults, explicit sexual requests, and money-scam wording, review blocked text and reports, verify profiles, investigate harassment, scams, and other rule-breaking, and suspend or remove accounts.
  4. Show local sponsored ads: choose which "Sponsored ad" to show (including "Plan your first date" suggestions from paying businesses, which are also labeled "Sponsored ad") based on your profile city and the screen you are on only, limit how often you see each ad, and give advertisers aggregate statistics. We do not use your gender, preferences, messages, photos, likes, or other profile details to choose ads, and ads never affect matching or the order of profiles.
  5. Handle sponsorship inquiries: respond to businesses that want to advertise.
  6. Protect and fix the Service: secure accounts, prevent fraud and abuse, debug problems, and improve features.
  7. Communicate with you: send account emails (such as sign-up verification and password reset) and respond to your questions.
  8. Follow the law: comply with legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, and enforce our Terms of Use.

We do not use your information for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. How information is shared

4.1 With other members

Your profile (display name, age, city, gender, dating intentions, bio, prompts and answers, interests, photos, and verified badge if you have one) is visible to other signed-in members who can see you in Discover or Browse. Members you match with can see the messages you send them and, if read receipts are on, whether you have read their messages. Members in a chat can see when you are typing. Plans you create, and attendance counts, are visible to other members. Your photo verification selfie, email address, birth date, reports, and blocks are not shown to other members.

If you delete your account, messages you already sent stay in the other person's chat (unless that chat was removed as described in Section 7), shown under the name "Deleted member." Text blocked by the content filter is never shown to other members.

Push notifications for new messages may show the sender's display name on the recipient's lock screen (for example, "Message from Alex"). Message text is not included in push notifications.

4.2 With service providers

We use a small number of service providers who process information on our behalf and only as needed to provide their services:

  • Supabase — hosting, database, authentication, file storage (photos and verification selfies), and server functions, in the hosting region configured for the FreeSpark project.
  • Apple — the App Store and the Apple Push Notification service (APNs), which delivers push notifications to your device.

4.3 With advertisers

Advertisers receive only aggregate statistics, such as the total number of impressions and taps for their promotion, by placement and time period. Advertisers do not receive your name, account ID, profile, photos, messages, city-level identity, or any information that identifies you. If you tap an ad that opens the advertiser's website or app, the advertiser's own privacy policy applies to anything you do there.

4.4 For legal and safety reasons

We may disclose information if we believe in good faith it is reasonably necessary to: comply with law, a court order, subpoena, or other legal process; respond to a valid request from law enforcement; protect the safety of any person; prevent fraud, abuse, or harm; or protect the rights and property of FreeSpark and our members.

4.5 Business transfers

If FreeSpark is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Privacy Policy or a notice to you.

4.6 With your consent

We may share information for other purposes if you ask us to or agree.

4.7 No selling or "sharing"

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined by California law. We have not done so in the past 12 months.

5. Who can see your messages and reports

We do not routinely read your private messages. FreeSpark's administrator may access messages, profiles, and related records when reasonably needed to investigate a report, enforce our Terms of Use, protect someone's safety, or comply with the law. Reports are reviewed by FreeSpark's administrator. The person you report is not told who reported them. When a chat is part of a report, a copy of that chat — including both people's messages — may be saved in a restricted safety archive that only FreeSpark's administrator can read (see Section 7). Text blocked by the automatic content filter is also saved for the administrator's safety review.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete my account. You can also email us at freesparkapp@gmail.com from the email address on your account.

When you delete your account:

  • Your login, profile, photos, prompts, likes and passes, matches, plans you created, attendance records, saved items, settings, notifications, and push tokens are deleted.
  • Chats with other members:
    • Chats that were part of a report (made by you or about you) are copied into a restricted safety archive that only FreeSpark's administrator can read. The copy includes the messages of both people, including the reported person's messages.
    • Other chats stay available to the remaining member. Messages you already sent remain in their chat history, and you are shown as "Deleted member" instead of your name and photo.
    • A chat that both people have left and that was never reported is removed.
  • Safety reports are kept. If you reported someone, or someone reported you, a copy of the report (display names, reason, details written in the report, status, and dates) is kept in the safety archive so we can protect other members and meet legal obligations.
  • Text you submitted that was blocked by the content filter may be kept for safety review (see Section 6).
  • Usage events are disconnected from your account.
  • Data may remain in backups for a limited time (see Section 6).
  • Deletion cannot be undone.

Accounts that manage advertising records (for example, an administrator account) cannot be deleted automatically in the app. If this applies to you, contact us and we will handle it.

We may also delete accounts that have been inactive for 2 years, after notice by email.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS), database access rules that limit each member to the data they are allowed to see, private storage for photos with short-lived links, and restricting administrator tools to a single authorized account. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of your information. Please use a strong, unique password and contact us right away if you think your account has been compromised. If a data breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by law.

9. Children

FreeSpark is only for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. We ask for your date of birth at sign-up and block anyone under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we will remove it and delete their information. If you believe a minor is using FreeSpark, please report the profile in the app or email freesparkapp@gmail.com.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Know and access the personal information we have about you.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Delete your personal information.
  • Get a copy of your information in a portable format.
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information, sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising, or targeted advertising. (We do not do these things.)
  • Not be discriminated against for using your rights.

To make a request, email freesparkapp@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy Request." We will verify your identity, usually by checking that you control the email address on your account. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof of the agent's authority and verify your identity directly. We will respond within the time required by law (for California, generally 45 days, which may be extended once by another 45 days when reasonably necessary). If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision with the subject "Privacy Appeal."
